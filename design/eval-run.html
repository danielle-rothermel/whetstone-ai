Inputs fixed before effects

Candidate and graph

The candidate artifact, native Graph Hash, and exact provider and evaluation-procedure configuration are fixed.

Evaluation contract

Eval Config, ordered task and repeat plan, Evaluation Role, purpose, and authority requirements are explicit.

Effect scope

Run, step, intent, route, and physical durability mode identify where every paid effect belongs.

Execution sequence

  1. Validate the complete intent and persist its claim.
  2. Resolve the Graph Config and verify its native Graph Hash.
  3. Expand the declared task and repeat plan without inferring planned rows from observed output.
  4. Execute provider calls through bounded policy and retain typed attempt evidence.
  5. Run preprocessing, metric extraction, and aggregation through dr-code contracts.
  6. Persist either complete outputs or identity-rich failure evidence.
  7. Bind the terminal evidence only while holding the current ownership generation.

Authority and publication

Official evaluation is an execution-time authority decision. Matching candidate or configuration hashes cannot transform internal evidence into official evidence.

An official evaluation must use the configured authority, carry the official role throughout intent and evidence, omit training Reward, and account for every planned row. The viewer receives a projection only after the runner re-resolves candidate, graph, Eval Config, task plan, role, authority, evidence, aggregate, and output references.

Acceptance evidence

  • Success covers the full planned matrix with no missing, failed, or invalid rows.
  • Failure evidence independently identifies the exact intent, route, role, plan, and effect scope.
  • Repeated execution resolves the existing terminal evidence or performs a safely owned attempt.
  • Published summaries retain the evaluation context needed to audit their authority.
